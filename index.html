Demonlist Rules

Rules for submitting completions and keeping the list fair.

Completion requirements

01

Complete the level from 0–100%

Practice runs, start-position runs, segmented runs, and percentage-only progress do not count as completions.

02

No cheating

Noclip, speedhacks, macros, bots, hitbox abuse, or any other tool that makes the completion illegitimate is not allowed.

03

Provide proof when requested

A video is strongly recommended for every completion and may be required for high placements, disputed runs, or new players.

04

The level must be unchanged

Copies are allowed only when the gameplay is identical to the original. Nerfed, altered, or easier versions do not count.

05

Allowed quality-of-life mods only

Mods such as FPS bypass, click-between-frames, texture packs, and visual fixes are allowed as long as they do not automate gameplay or reduce difficulty.

06

One record per player per level

Each player can earn points for a level once. Rebeats can replace an old video but do not give extra points.

07

Rankings are decided by the group

Placements are based on the opinions and experiences of local players. Levels may move when more people beat them or the group agrees the order is wrong.

08

Be honest and respectful

Fake claims, stolen videos, harassment, or repeated arguments over placements can result in a record being rejected or removed.

Final decisions: The list owner may ask for extra proof, reject suspicious runs, or update these rules when needed.
